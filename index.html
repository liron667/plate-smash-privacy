Opa! Plate Smash – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Opa! Plate Smash (“the game”, “we”) is a free casual game for Android published by Liron Barel. This policy explains what information the game uses and how.

Information we collect

The game has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of its own. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Your game progress (high scores, coins, upgrades, selected plate design, and the name you may type for the on-device leaderboard) is saved only on your device and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes it.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded video ads (for example, to get one more chance after losing) using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use, on your device:

  • your Android advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about how you interact with ads and the app,
  • crash logs and diagnostic/performance data.

This data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy. See also how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). The game has no other analytics or tracking SDKs.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Data security and retention

We do not store your data on any server. Data collected by our advertising partner is transmitted over encrypted connections and retained according to its policies.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the latest version.
