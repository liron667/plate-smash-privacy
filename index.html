Opa! Plate Smash – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Opa! Plate Smash (“the game”, “we”) is a free casual game for Android published by Liron Barel. This policy explains what information the game uses and how.

Information we collect

The game has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of its own. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Your game progress (high scores, coins, upgrades, selected plate design, and the name you may type for the on-device leaderboard) is saved only on your device and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes it.

Advertising and tracking

The game does not show ads, does not use analytics, and does not include third-party tracking SDKs.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Data security and retention

We do not store your data on any server, so there is nothing for us to retain or share.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the latest version.
